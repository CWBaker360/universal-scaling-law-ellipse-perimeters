# Abstract

For the equal-parameter inscribed polygon of a nondegenerate ellipse, the
polygonal perimeter factors exactly into a universal sinc term and a composite
midpoint approximation to the ellipse speed. Periodic analytic quadrature
makes the midpoint error exponentially small. Therefore all algebraic
relative-error coefficients are universal, and a one-step tripling correction
cancels the inverse-square term and gives fourth-order convergence.

Eccentricity enters through the analytic strip width
\(\operatorname{artanh}(b/a)\), which controls the onset of the asymptotic
regime.
